# Eight-level world progression

All ten options now reach level 8. Choosing an option places level 1 and automatically resolves every eligible upgrade across all placed options. Each gained level receives its own reveal. Earlier improvements remain visible as later regions appear.

Levels 1–3 establish the castle town and independent districts outside its walls. Market, Workshop, Roads, Archive and Observatory keep their original sites, gain permanent local additions through level 8, and connect to the castle when developed. Levels 4–8 add fifty projects across the valley, foothills and mountains. Roads follow the ground, forests use reserved planting sites, and river projects excavate terrain before animated water fills their channels. Skip and reduced motion finish construction immediately; loading a save restores completed projects.

## Connections

The original level 2–3 prerequisites and sealed-wall consequence remain in [current progression](current-progression.md). For levels 4–8, both partners below must reach the target level minus two. The world must also contain the listed number of chosen ideas. Prerequisites always point to lower levels, so higher-level dependencies cannot form a deadlock.

## Validation

Progression, save migration, automatic upgrade beats, cumulative reveals, excavation timing, reset behavior and shared material ownership are covered by automated checks in desktop and mobile detail modes. Runtime geometry is exported with `node --import tsx scripts/export-town-review.mjs --game --frontier` and reviewed with `art/blender/review_frontier.py`. The water effect is an animated surface and advancing flow front, not a fluid dynamics simulation.
