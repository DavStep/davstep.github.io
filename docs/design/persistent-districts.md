# Choices build places that grow

A click constructs the chosen component immediately, including when prerequisites block its next upgrade. Market builds Idle Outpost northwest of the castle. Its original trading post remains in the same place while subsequent choices improve it and add neighboring market buildings.

| Market level | Change at Idle Outpost |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Trading post, first market building, tavern and courtyard |
| 2 | Improved post, merchant banners and supplies |
| 3 | Full trading hall, street lights and castle road |
| 4 | Covered stalls |
| 5 | Merchant warehouse |
| 6 | Artisan bazaar |
| 7 | Trade office |
| 8 | Grand trading hall and formal entrance |

Workshop/Sandship, the dwarven roadworks, Archive and Wizard Observatory also occupy permanent districts outside the castle. Their eight levels improve the original buildings, add supporting buildings, and preserve earlier construction. Homes remain associated with the castle; the existing mill, grove and waterways retain their established sites.

District roads connect to the existing cardinal gates and moat crossings. Market, Workshop, Archive and Observatory need their own level 3 and Roads 2. The dwarven roadworks connects at Roads 2. A sealed wall still blocks the castle connection. Local buildings remain visible when disconnected.

Automatic progression still retries every placed option after each choice. The camera returns to the original district for its upgrades; frontier developments remain additional effects of the same level. Old saves recompute at the new fixed sites without changing their chosen order.

Validation covers Market as the first choice, all five fixed district centers, cumulative local buildings through level 8, connection prerequisites and sealed gates, plus desktop/mobile reset and resource ownership. Blender reviews use the actual exported runtime meshes; see `art/reviews/districts/market-first.png`, `market-grown.png` and `connected-districts.png`.
