# Living world backdrop

These two original backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted to optimized WebP. They sit behind the live SVG village, weather, travelers, and seasonal terrain.

- `twilight-valley-illustrated.webp`: ultra-wide illustrated twilight valley; dark navy open sky for page copy, layered slate mountains, winding river, teal hills, coral horizon, no buildings or characters.
- `winter-valley-illustrated.webp`: edit of the same image preserving the composition while adding snow, frost, and a colder blue-silver palette.

## Final prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Both outputs were converted to WebP at quality 80.

### Twilight valley

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: original illustrated panoramic backdrop for a live interactive 2D fantasy village simulation on a personal portfolio
> Primary request: a visually striking handcrafted game environment plate, NOT photorealistic. There will be separately animated castle, houses, trees, travelers and weather placed in front of this image; include NO buildings, NO close trees, NO people, NO animals, NO text.
> Scene/backdrop: very wide twilight valley with a winding pale river, multiple atmospheric ranges of blue slate mountains, rolling muted teal hills, delicate warm coral light on the horizon, tiny firefly glints. Upper half has elegant open navy sky for large white website headline on the left.
> Style/medium: premium 2D animation background, bold layered silhouette design with subtle hand-painted surface texture and soft edge light, somewhat like an art-directed indie game, visible illustration strokes, clean geometry, stylized rather than realistic; dimensional but not 3D-rendered, no pixel art.
> Composition/framing: ultra-wide horizontal panorama approximately 2.7:1, horizon at lower half, left side dark and low-detail for typography, most dramatic ridge forms on right, lower middle relatively open for live castle overlay, no framing or vignette.
> Color palette: ink navy, desaturated indigo, jade-teal, slate blue, pale mauve and coral peach; distinct contrast, no muddy brown, no bright neon.
> Avoid: photographic mountain textures, generic concept-art realism, castles, houses, foreground trees, people, animals, lettering, UI, borders, stars drawn as symbols.

### Winter edit

> Use case: lighting-weather
> Asset type: winter variant of the exact same interactive website background plate
> Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve its ultra-wide framing, camera position, mountain shapes, river route, valley contours, open dark upper-left sky, and overall illustrated 2D game-art style.
> Primary request: change the season to a believable magical winter twilight. Add snow blankets across the valley floor and mountain shoulders, cold blue-silver atmospheric haze, frozen or partly frozen reflective river, delicate frost texture and faint drifting snow in the distance. Keep a subtle pale mauve-coral glow at the horizon so it still belongs to the same world.
> Constraints: preserve all landscape geometry, keep lower middle open for live castle overlay, no buildings, no characters, no foreground trees, no text, no UI, no new major landforms. Stay illustrated rather than photographic. High quality and crisp at website scale.
